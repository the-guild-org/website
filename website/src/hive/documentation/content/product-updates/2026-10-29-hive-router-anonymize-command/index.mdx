[Hive Router](/docs/router) `v0.3.8` adds an `anonymize` command, so you can share a supergraph and its
operations, in a bug report or with support, without revealing your schema.

```bash
hive_router anonymize keygen > anon.key
hive_router anonymize supergraph --key-file anon.key supergraph.graphql > anonymous.graphql
hive_router anonymize operation --key-file anon.key operation.graphql > anonymous-operation.graphql
```

Every type, field, argument, enum value, subgraph name, string and number is replaced with a hash,
and descriptions are removed. The output is still a valid supergraph that the router plans the same
way, just with names like `T_3f9a…` and `f_81c2…` instead of `User` and `email`.

<Callout type="info">
  Hit a bug or a slow query plan? Anonymize the supergraph and the operation,
  and attach them to an issue in the [Hive Router
  repository](https://github.com/graphql-hive/router/issues). We get a working
  reproduction, and none of your sensitive or internal information leaves your
  hands (keep the key to yourself!)
</Callout>
