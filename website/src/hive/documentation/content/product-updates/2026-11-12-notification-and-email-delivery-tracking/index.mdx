Hive sends a lot on your behalf: schema change notifications and metric alerts to Slack, Microsoft
Teams, Discord and webhooks, and emails for invitations, ownership transfers, audit log exports and
usage limits. Until now, a delivery that failed was invisible. Starting today, every attempt is
recorded and shown in Hive Console, together with the answer the destination gave.

<Image
  alt="Alert channel page with delivery stats, a chart over time, the channel's health and the delivery log"
  src={channelPageImage}
/>

## Why it matters

Notifications and emails are delivered by a background worker, after the request that caused them
has already returned. A schema publish succeeds whether or not the Slack message about it was
posted, and an invitation is created whether or not the email went out. There was never a place to
surface a failure.

These deliveries also rely on configuration that lives outside Hive and can be invalidated without
anyone touching Hive:

- A Slack token is revoked, the app is uninstalled, a channel is archived, or the Hive bot is
  removed from a private channel.
- A Microsoft Teams or Discord incoming webhook is deleted.
- A webhook endpoint is decommissioned, moves, or starts timing out.
- A mail provider refuses an address as unknown or inactive.

Before, you found out when a breaking change reached production without the announcement, or when
a new teammate never got their invitation. Now Hive shows you, and flags the channel first.

## Every alert channel has a page

Click a channel on a project's **Alerts** page to see its total calls and failures over the last 72
hours, a chart of deliveries over time, and a log of the most recent attempts: the event, the
target, how long the call took, which retry it was, and the status the destination answered, such
as `200 OK`, `404 Not Found` or `Timed out`. Expand an attempt to see the exact headers Hive sent
(`Hive-Event`, `Hive-Target-ID`, `Hive-Schema-ID`, `Idempotency-Key`), so you can match it to a
request in your own logs. Payloads and response bodies are never stored.

## Failing channels are flagged, then paused

Each channel now has a health status: **Healthy**, **Failing** when its ten most recent attempts
all failed, or **No data**. After an hour of failing, the channel page shows a warning with the last
response.

<Image
  alt="A channel that has been failing for more than a day, with the warning and the last response"
  src={channelFailingImage}
/>

After 48 hours of failing, Hive pauses the channel so a dead destination does not keep absorbing
notifications. The page says when and why, and an **Enable channel** button brings it back once the
destination is fixed. You can also pause a channel yourself with the **Channel status** switch, and
**Edit channel** lets you point it at a new Slack channel or URL without recreating it.

## Email deliveries for your organization

The organization settings gain an **Email Deliveries** section: how many emails the mail provider
accepted or refused in the last 72 hours, a chart over time, and a log with the recipient, the
provider's answer and the retry count. Members see the kinds of email of the features they can
manage, so someone who can invite members sees invitations, and someone who can export audit logs
sees the export emails.

<Image
  alt="The Email Deliveries section of the organization settings"
  src={emailDeliveriesImage}
/>

Hive retries an email up to five times when the provider is rate limited or unavailable, and stops
at once when the provider refuses the recipient, because an inactive address will not become active
on the fourth try.

## Invitation status at a glance

The invitations table has a new **Email status** column: **Sent** once the mail provider accepted
the email, **Failed (attempt n of m)** with the provider's answer and whether Hive will try again,
or **No delivery record** before the first attempt. If an email cannot be delivered, copy the
invitation link from the row menu and share it directly.

<Image
  alt="The invitations table with the Email status column"
  src={invitationsStatusImage}
/>

## Get started

Open any channel from your project's **Alerts** page, or **Settings → Email Deliveries** in your
organization. Self-hosting Hive? The `workflows` service records its deliveries when its ClickHouse
connection is configured (`CLICKHOUSE=1`).
