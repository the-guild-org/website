Metric Alerts, which we [introduced in July](/product-updates/2026-07-24-metric-alerts), are now
available to every organization by default. There's no longer anything to ask us to enable, and that
includes self-hosted Hive Console.

Metric Alerts watch your GraphQL API's live operational metrics (total requests, error rate, and
latency) over a rolling range on any target. When a rule crosses a fixed threshold, or swings by a
percentage against the previous window, Hive notifies you in Slack, MS Teams, or a webhook. Rules
move through a **Normal → Pending → Firing → Recovering** lifecycle, so you only get paged on the
transitions that matter.

## Get started

Open any target, head to its **Alerts** section, and create your first rule. If you run into any
issues, please reach out to us.
